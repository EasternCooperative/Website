description: |-
  Join us for ECRS Fun Day in Philly on Saturday, October 17! 🎉 at the [Philadelphia Ethical Society](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Philadelphia+Ethical+Society/data=!4m2!3m1!1s0x0:0x9b32d5a0ad5a0242?sa=X&ved=1t:2428&ictx=111) in Center City, Philadelphia.

  Enjoy a mix of full-group activities and two workshop periods, with choices including folk and line dancing, community singing, contra & community dances, crafts, cooperative games, and theater exercises. There will be activities for the whole family, with plenty of opportunities to learn something new, meet people, and have fun together. We’ll come back together for a potluck dinner, followed by an evening of more group activities and singing.

  See schedule, below.

  ---

  All ages, experience levels, and physical abilities are welcome.

  Come for whole day or just part of the day. Bring comfortable shoes and a willingness to try new things!

  (Optional) Bring snacks or a potluck item to share.

  ---

  Parking - Parking discounts are available for the garage @ Parkway [1845 Walnut Street](https://www.parkwaycorp.com/parking/locations/1845-walnut-street/philadelphia/pa/19103). We will provide the vouchers at registration, first come first serve. Please check the box in the "questions" section. Street parking is available. There are many [parking garages](https://www.google.com/maps/search/parking+garages+near+the+ethical+society+philadelphia/) around Rittenhouse Square.

  Public Transportation is easily available. The Ethical Society is a 10 minute walk from City Hall. Check SEPTA & PATCO website for more information.
