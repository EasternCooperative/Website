  ECRS Summer School is a joyous parade of opportunities for play and rejuvenation through creativity. Our unique approach offers an opportunity to learn recreation leadership by participation rather than formal discussion, and attendance is open to members and non-members alike.

  Powell House is a Quaker conference and retreat center in Old Chatham, New York, in the foothills of the Berkshires overlooking the Hudson River Valley, with 52 acres of lawns, gardens, woods, and a pond. Staff coordinator: Ruth Sherman.
