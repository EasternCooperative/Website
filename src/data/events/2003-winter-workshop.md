  Winter Workshop is a creative, joyous, and alternative holiday experience: an array of well-led classes scheduled to leave room for breaks and free time, three buffet meals a day, and evenings of singing, cooperative games, dancing, and special theme nights. The week culminates in welcoming the New Year with song, dance, and ceremony.

  Staff coordinator: Ruth Sherman. Onsite business manager: Lee Friedman.
