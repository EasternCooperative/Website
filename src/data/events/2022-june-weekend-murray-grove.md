Murray Grove Retreat and Renewal Center is a 20-acre partially wooded campus with a history of over 240 years, boasting an outdoor in-ground salt-water pool, an eleven-circuit labyrinth, and walking trails in a 200-acre wildlife preserve immediately adjacent.
