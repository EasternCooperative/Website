  The Camping Weekend is a time for people of all ages and walks of life, from newcomers to old-timers, to share in fun and fellowship. Daytime classes are a chance to enjoy selected activities, while the Evening Program on Friday and Saturday night brings everyone together as a community to dance, sing, play games, and socialize over snacks.
