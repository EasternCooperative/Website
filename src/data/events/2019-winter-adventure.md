  ECRS Winter Adventure is a creative and joyous alternative holiday experience. Young and old alike enjoy a diverse array of classes (improv to introspection, crafts to collaborating games, and much more), plus breaks and free time for relaxing or leading your own recreational activities. A dedicated children's program makes sure our youngest members have as much fun as the grown-ups during class times.

  This year introduced "pay-what-you-can" pricing for room and board — the listed price is the break-even cost, and attendees were invited to pay more to support others or less if it would strain their budget.

  Watson Homestead is a conference center on 600 beautiful acres near Corning, NY, wheelchair accessible and ideally situated for winter sports and local attractions including sledding, hiking, and the nearby Corning Museum of Glass.
