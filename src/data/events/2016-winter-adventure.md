    period: 'Morning, second period'
    days: '4 days'
    description: "What is decoupage? The art and craft of the papercutter, who uses paper 'like a writer uses words' to create something new, adhering with glue any of an endless variety of papers to a new surfaces. Your imagination creates the visuals and in the class we will work with the techniques of gluing, layering, sanding and sealing, using hairdryers to aid the drying process. Become an 'image pack rat', focusing on a subject as you gather what catches your eyes from calendars, magazines, wallpaper and paint samples, flower and travel catalogs, family photo albums. A variety of boxes, trays, etc., in paper, wood, metal will be provided. You can bring your own, too, as well as scissors, exacto knives, mat knives with snap-off blades, and your curiosity, creativity and enthusiasm."
