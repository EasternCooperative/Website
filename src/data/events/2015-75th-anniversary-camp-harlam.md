  A long weekend of fun for the whole family at Camp Harlam, celebrating 75 years of creating community, making new friends, and developing leadership through play and the arts. Camp Harlam is in the foothills of the Pocono Mountains, about two hours from Philadelphia and New York City, with over 300 acres and heated facilities.

  About 100 people of all ages came. One participant later wrote: "With a group of 100 intergenerational people, a lot of fun ideas were shared. I think it was the fact that 100% of the people who attended were so much fun to be with." They remembered games like Mafia, Psychiatrist, and Spit, magic tricks, improv games like "Why Were You Late" and "Three Headed Expert," dancing the Stockton Goralski and the Jiffy Mixer, and a chance to hear and see the history of ECRS.
