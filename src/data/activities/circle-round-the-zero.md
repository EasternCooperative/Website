origin: 'From the book *Circle Round the Zero*, street chants and games collected by Maureen Kenney. Reprinted in Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990).'
instructions: |
  Players stand in a circle clapping the beat, spaced slightly apart from each other. The leader walks around the outside of the circle.

  - At "Back, back, zero" the leader stands back to back with one of the players.
  - At "Side, side, zero" the leader stands beside that player.
  - At "Front, front, zero" the leader stands facing that player.
  - At "Tap your lovin' zero" the leader taps that player on the shoulders.

  The game is repeated with the chosen player as leader.
lyrics: |
  Circle 'round the zero,
  Find your lovin' zero,
  Back, back, zero,
  Side, side, zero,
  Front, front, zero,
  Tap your lovin' zero.
