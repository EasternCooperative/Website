title: 'Carabamboli'

excerpt: 'The "Israeli Virginia Reel": a quickly learned longways dance where head and foot couples slide the set and everyone casts off under an arch.'
origin: 'Israel. The lilting tune is universal, with Danish and American counterparts and French roots. Contributed by Ruth Sherman to Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990).'
instructions: |
  | Measures | |
  | --- | --- |
  | 1-4 | As the others clap in time to the rhythm, the head couple takes hands and slides down to the bottom of the set in 8 counts. |
  | 5-8 | Return the same way to place. |
  | 9-12 | Foot couple does the same (slides up the set). |
  | 13-16 | Return. |
  | 17-24 | All face front and the head couple casts out (away from partner) while the others in line follow, as they form an arch at the bottom of the set. The rest of the lines duck under the arch as they meet their partner, take hands, and are led by the original 2nd couple back to the top of the set. The head couple remains at the foot of the set. |

  The dance starts again with a new head couple, and the "old" head couple participates by being the new foot couple.
leadingTips: 'This happy dance is quickly learned and is "sure-fire" with any group (Ruth Sherman).'
formation: 'Longways set'
groupSizeAdaptationNotes: 'Best done with 8 couples to a set. It can be a slower paced dance with 5-6 couples, or faster moving with 9-10 couples.'
materialsNeeded: 'Music: "Folk Dances and Festive Dances" (BAN 14105 LP). The record goes through only 4 times, so it needs to be put on again for 8 couples, or a double-length tape can be made.'
