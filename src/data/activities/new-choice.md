origin: 'Improv folk tradition'
relatedLeaderIds: ['isaac-lebwohl-steiner']
instructions: |
  Performers start a scene, with or without a prompt. Now and then, right after
  a performer makes a choice in the scene, the leader calls "New Choice!" The
  performer immediately makes a different choice in its place and the scene
  continues.

  For example, "I'm going golfing with my uncle next weekend" might become "I'm
  going skydiving with my uncle next weekend," and if the leader calls "New
  Choice!" again, "We're going to the moon with my uncle right now!" Each choice
  should be bolder or bigger than the last.

  The scene runs to its natural conclusion, with the leader interjecting from
  time to time.
leadingTips: |
  Be careful how often you call "New Choice!" Let the scene keep its momentum
  and make progress. Only stack a double "New Choice!" a couple of times per
  scene.
