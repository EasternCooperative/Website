origin: 'Contributed by Jay Goldspinner to Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990).'
instructions: 'The leader tells a short folktale (for example, "Bye, Bye" or "The Difference Between Heaven and Hell") in its bare-bones, simplest form. Then people retell it in their own style, details, and approach, in pairs, to each other. Then one or more volunteers tell the story they just told in the pairs to the whole group.'
leadingTips: "Jay Goldspinner mixes and matches these storytelling activities over a series of sessions, encouraging people to find their own way of telling and their own stories. There really is no wrong way of doing it. It is most important for the leader to create an atmosphere of safety, especially by initially setting criteria for feedback that respect people's vulnerability and differences."
