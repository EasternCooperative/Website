instructions: |
  **Formation:** Single circle.

  *First stanza* ("Shoo fly, don't bother me...."):

  1. Hands joined, all take four steps forward.
  2. Take four steps backward to place.
  3. Repeat forward four steps.
  4. Take four steps backward to place.

  *Second stanza* ("I do, I do, I do...."): Keep hands joined to turn the circle inside out. A couple on the opposite side of the circle makes an arch for a couple opposite them to lead the circle under the arch. The couple making the arch turns under their own arm.

  *First stanza:* Take four steps backward toward the center of the circle and four steps out. (Repeat.)

  *Second stanza:* The lead couple goes back up under the arch made by the opposite couple to turn the circle right side out.

  For I belong to somebody,
  Yes, indeed I do.
