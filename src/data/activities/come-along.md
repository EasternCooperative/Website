title: 'Come Along'
type: 'game'
draft: false
excerpt: '"It" gathers a growing line of players from the circle while the music plays; when it stops, everyone dashes for a seat.'
origin: 'Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990)'
objective: 'To get a chair when the music stops.'
instructions: |
  **Formation:** Players seated in a circle. An extra person who doesn't have a seat. At least 12 or 15 players are needed, and it can be played with a large group.

  When the music starts, "it" walks around inside the circle, close to the seated players, extending a hand toward players. Any player may take "it's" hand: that player then holds out a hand to pick up another player. Each added player picks up another, until the music stops. At that point all hurry to find a seat. The player left without a seat is the new "it" and play begins again.
variations: |
  If the circle is large, two or more "its" may start a line.

  If music is not available, the leader may start and stop the action using a bell, tambourine, or whistle.
formation: 'Circle of chairs'
groupSizeMin: 12
materialsNeeded: 'Lively taped or recorded music (or a bell, tambourine, or whistle), and a chair for every player but one.'
