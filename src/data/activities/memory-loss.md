instructions: |
  Participants sit in pairs, facing each other. Each pair decides who tells the
  first story, or the leader sets the order. The storyteller begins an
  improvised story and, after one to three sentences, pretends to forget what
  comes next, trailing off with "and... and...".

  For example: "I was walking down the street carrying my groceries when I saw
  a dog. The dog ran up to me and... and...".

  The partner fills the gap with an offer, for example "...and stood up on its
  hind legs and grabbed your baguette!" The storyteller accepts whatever the
  partner said and keeps going: "Right! It stood up and took my bread. It was
  crazy. It ran off with it, I chased after it, and then... then...".

  This continues until the story ends on its own or the leader calls for a
  switch. Play continues until both partners have told a story, or until the
  leader ends the activity. You can run more rounds if the energy is right.
leadingTips: |
  Demonstrate the game in front of the group first as a model.

  Make sure pairs are far enough apart that everyone can hear, taking the
  acoustics of the room into account.
