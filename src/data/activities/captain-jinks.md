instructions: |
  **Formation:** Couples in a single circle, facing in, women to the right of their partners.

  1. Clap hands.
  2. All bow to partners.
  3. Join hands in a complete circle and march to the center and back twice.
  4. Gentleman crosses in front of his own partner and takes the partner of the man on his right.
  5. Swing the lady once around and keep her for a new partner.
  6. Promenade counter-clockwise until the song is finished. Then start over.

adaptations: |
  **In wheelchairs** (chair and pusher equal two people): wheelchairs in a circle facing the center, each pusher standing on the left side of a wheelchair.

  - "When Captain Jinks comes home at night, he claps his hands with all his might": all clap hands four times.
  - "Salute your partner if you're not too green, for that's the style in the army": bow to your partner.
  - "All join hands and forward all... backward all": push chairs to the center four steps and back four steps.
  - "When Captain Jinks comes home at night, the pusher passes to the right": pusher goes to the right, to a new chair.
  - "Swing that partner so polite": swing.
  - "Promenade all around the hall": push the chair in a promenade around the ring.

  Repeat until original partners return to each other. (From Eddie Moyer's wheelchair dances in the book's Special Populations chapter.)
