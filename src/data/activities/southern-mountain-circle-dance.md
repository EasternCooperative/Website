title: 'Southern Mountain Circle Dance'

excerpt: 'A leisurely, sociable big-circle dance where active couples progress around the ring doing called square dance figures with each new couple.'
origin: 'Southern Mountain circle dancing, as Ed Moyer led it. Written up in Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990).'
instructions: |
  **Formation:** Circle of couples. A promenade, or a single line walking to music, can be used to get into formation. Two couples face each other in the circle, and the couples moving counter-clockwise are called number one, or active, couples. The active couples progress at the end of each figure to a new "number two" couple.

  **Big circle figures**

  - "All join hands and balance all." (Balance in.)
  - "If you like that do it again." (Everybody into the center four steps and back. Repeat.)
  - "Ladies in." (Right-hand person into the center four steps and back.)
  - "Gents in." (Left-hand person does the same.)
  - "Swing your partner." (Swing partner in a buzz step swing.)
  - "Swing your corner." (Turn away from your partner and swing the person you're facing.)
  - "Circle right." (All take hands in the circle and move right.)
  - "Circle left." (Circle moves left.)

  **Figures for two couples**

  *Ladies Chain*

  - "Four hands up and away you go." (Circle left, four people in a circle.)
  - "Head right back in the way you know." (Circle right.)
  - "Ladies chain across the set." (Right-hand persons give right hands across to each other, passing right shoulders; the opposite left-hand person meets her with the left hand and turns her into place.) Repeat the call and action, chaining back to partners.
  - "Circle four in the middle of the floor." (All four circle.)
  - "Swing the opposite." "Swing your own."
  - "On to the next." (Number one couples move to the next.)

  *Right Hand Star*

  - "All join hands and circle left." (Two couples join hands and circle left.)
  - "Right hand star and how are you." (All four put right hands into the center and continue moving in the same direction.)
  - "Left hand back." (Put left hands into the center and reverse direction.)
  - "Corners swing." (Swing your opposite.) "Now swing your own." (Swing your partner.)
  - "On to the next." (Move to the next couple.)

  *Right and Left Through*

  - "A right and left through." (Two couples cross over to each other's place, the right-hand persons passing left shoulders and the left-hand persons keeping to the outside. The left-hand person takes partner's left hand and backs her around in place with right hand on her right hand at the hip.)
  - "And a right and left back." (Repeat the figure to get back to place.)
  - "Now circle left in a little ring." (All four circle.)
  - "Break that ring with a corner swing." (Swing the corner.)
  - "Swing your own and move on." (Swing your partner and move to the next couple.)

  *Take a Little Peek*

  - "Around that couple and take a little peek." (Couple 1 peeks around couple 2.)
  - "Back to the center and swing your sweet." (Return to place and swing.)
  - "Around that couple and peek once more." (Couple 1 peeks around couple 2.)
  - "Back to the center and swing all four." (Both couples swing and move to the next couple.)

  *Bird in the Cage*

  - "Circle four in the middle of the floor." (All four circle left.)
  - "Bird in the cage and three hands round." (Number one right-hand person in the center; the other three circle around.)
  - "Bird hop out and crow hop in. Caw, caw." (Left-hand person steps into the circle, right-hand person out, and three circle around.)
  - "Crow hop out, goin' again." (Left-hand person steps out and joins partner in the circle.)
  - "Swing your opposite." "Swing your own." (On to the next couple.)

  Any of these figures can be repeated, as the caller wishes. End the dance with one or more of the big circle calls.
leadingTips: 'The progression around the circle gives dancers a chance to meet and greet others, and the caller can vary the figures to the ability of the group. Ed Moyer started it by simply setting off with the music, holding the hand of the person behind him and taking big strides, until the straggly line formed into a circle.'
formation: 'Big circle of couples, two couples facing'
