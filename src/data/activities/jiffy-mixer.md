title: 'Jiffy Mixer'
type: 'dance'
draft: false
excerpt: 'A hugely popular American round-dance mixer: heel-and-toe, side-close, chug away with a clap, and walk on to a new partner.'
origin: 'USA round dance. Contributed by Ruth Sherman to Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990).'
instructions: |
  **Formation:** Double circle, all facing partners: men's backs to the center, women's backs to the wall, using opposite footwork.

  | Measures | |
  | --- | --- |
  | 1-4 | Men's left, women's right: heel out, foot replace; repeat. Side step to the same side, close the other foot. Repeat. |
  | 5-8 | Repeat the above with opposite footwork. |
  | 9-12 | All chug away from partners with 4 chugs (a low movement with both feet moving together): chug, pause and clap (4 times), the claps coming on each pause, so on the off-beat. |
  | 13-16 | All move to the left in four easy steps, coming to a new partner who is meeting you half-way, also doing 4 easy steps. |

  Begin the dance all over again.
leadingTips: "Extremely popular with all ages and levels of experience; the catchy tune and the invitation to add one's own styling create an upbeat mood. In a larger group people often request it again, and it is fun to keep going until people meet or pass their original partner, so everyone dances with at least half the people in the room."
formation: 'Double circle, partners facing'
