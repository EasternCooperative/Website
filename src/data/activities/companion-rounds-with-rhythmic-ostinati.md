instructions: |
  Divide the gathering into three groups. Assign to each group "Frere Jacques," "Row, Row, Row Your Boat," or "Three Blind Mice." Encourage each group to invent a short rhythmic body sound or movement pattern (an ostinato) to accompany their round. For example:

  1. Row, Row, Row Your Boat: clap, clap, snap, snap
  2. Frere Jacques: patch, (rest), patch, (rest) (a "patch" is a slap on the lap)
  3. Three Blind Mice: snap, snap, clap, (rest)

  Let each group do theirs, one at a time. Then starting with group 1, bring each group in until all are singing and doing their respective ostinati. End by stopping one group at a time.
