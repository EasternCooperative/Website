origin: "Norway (the book's sheet music marks the tune Norwegian). Written up in Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990)."
instructions: |
  **Formation:** Players take partners and stand in a single circle, facing the center. One player in the circle, without a partner, is the first "thief."

  To the music, the thief slides sideways across the circle and steals a partner from someone in the circle. Taking both hands of the new partner, the thief slides back across the circle to his or her original place. The player whose partner was stolen immediately slides sideways across the circle and takes some other player's partner, and they both slide back to place. This continues without interruption, the player whose partner has been stolen, in each case, immediately sliding sideways to secure another.
