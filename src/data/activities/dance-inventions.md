instructions: |
  **Song:** any familiar song. **Formation:** a standing circle, hands joined, with or without partners.

  1. Sing the song.
  2. Identify phrases (units that seem separable). For example: (a) Twinkle, twinkle, little star (b) How I wonder what you are (c) Up above the world so high (d) Like a diamond in the sky (e) Twinkle, twinkle, little star (f) How I wonder what you are.
  3. Encourage the group to select dance movements that will feel good in sequence, a different one for each phrase. For example, in the above song: (a) walk 4 steps clockwise (b) turn singly in place, counter-clockwise (c) walk 4 steps to the center (d) walk 4 steps back (e) walk 4 steps counter-clockwise (f) turn singly in place, clockwise.
variations: 'For a round, arrange the group in two or more concentric circles. Starting with the innermost, each circle begins and ends dancing and singing at the appropriate time.'
