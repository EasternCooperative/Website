origin: 'Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990)'
instructions: |
  **The call**

  **1.** All eight balance and all eight swing. A left allemande and a right hand grand. Meet your partner and promenade eight till you come straight.
  **2.** First couple balance and swing, out to the right.
  **3.** Four hands round.
  **4.** Leave that lady and on to the next.
  **5.** And three hands round.
  **6.** Take that lady and on to the next.
  **7.** Four hands round.
  **8.** Leave that lady and balance home.
  **9.** Forward up six,
  **10.** Fall back six.
  **11.** Forward up six and right and left through.
  **12.** Two lone gents across the hall.
  **13-16.** Forward up six, fall back six, forward up six and right and left through, two lone gents across the hall.
  **17.** Four on the corners here and there and do si do with the pretty little dear.
  **18.** Now you're home. All eight balance and all eight swing. A left allemande and a right hand grand. Meet your partner and promenade eight till you come straight.

  The second, third, and fourth couples repeat 2 to 18 in turn.

  **What the dancers do**

  **1.** All eight balance and all eight swing.
  **2.** Balance and swing. The first couple walks to the second couple.
  **3.** First and second couples join hands in a circle and walk to the left (8 counts).
  **4.** The first gentleman leaves his partner at the left of the second gentleman and walks to the third couple (4 counts).
  **5.** The first gentleman joins hands in a circle with the third couple, and they walk to the left (8 counts).
  **6.** Taking the third lady's left hand in his right, the first gentleman walks to the fourth couple (4 counts).
  **7.** These four join hands in a circle and walk to the left (8 counts).
  **8.** The first gentleman leaves the third lady at the left of the fourth gentleman and walks to his own place (4 counts).
  **9.** Second and fourth gentlemen take the ladies' hands, and both lines walk forward four steps.
  **10.** They walk directly backward four steps.
  **11.** The lines advance and, releasing hands, pass through, the two gentlemen keeping to the left in passing each other. All turn singly and stay on the opposite side of the set.
  **12.** The first and third gentlemen cross to each other's places, keeping to the left in passing (8 counts).
  **13-16.** Lines forward four steps and back, advance and pass through, and the first and third gentlemen cross again, as in 9-12.
  **17.** Each "lone gent" walks to the couple with whom his partner is dancing (4 counts), joins hands in a circle, and all do a "do si do," which brings all couples into their own places.
  **18.** All eight balance and swing.
formation: 'Square of four couples'
