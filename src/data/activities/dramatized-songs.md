instructions: |
  Dramatize the lyrics of a song familiar to the whole group. Choose a song that tells a story with form implicit in it: a beginning, a middle, and an end. Keep in mind good characterizations, action, and production possibilities.

  Any type of song can be used. Folk songs are especially adaptable, such as "Froggy Went A-Courtin'" and "On Top of Old Smokey." The song can be played and sung as the action takes place.
