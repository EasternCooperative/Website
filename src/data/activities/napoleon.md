origin: "Denmark (the book's sheet music marks the tune Danish). Written up in Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990)."
instructions: |
  Couples in a circle, men with backs to center of circle, partners facing. With joined hands, proceed to the man's left for 4 step-close steps in time to: "Na-po-leon *was* a mighty warrior." On "tra-la-la...," six slide steps to the man's right. Repeat the above for the next two lines.

  On the last three lines, couples in shoulder-waist position do 8 step-hops in a small circle, ending in the beginning formation. Each person takes one step to their own left, for a new partner.
lyrics: |
  1. Napoleon was a mighty warrior,
  Tra la la la la la, la.
  A great, big, bully, fighting terrier,
  Tra la la la la la, la.

  2. Oh, Boney fought the Rooshians,
  Oh, Boney fought the Prooshians,

  3. And Boney got capitulation.
