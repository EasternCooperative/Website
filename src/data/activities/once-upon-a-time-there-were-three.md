excerpt: 'Groups of three build a story on a fixed frame (three characters, a journey, a meeting, a conflict, a resolution) and tell it together.'
origin: 'Contributed by Jay Goldspinner to Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990).'
instructions: |
  The activity begins with this framework:

  - Once upon a time there were three (pigs, children, ants...)
  - Their names were:
  - They were on their way to (the grocery store, the moon, a palace...)
  - On the way they met (a dragon, a macho man, the Devil...)
  - (Have a real conflict develop.)
  - What happened? (Resolve the conflict.)

  Each group of three people makes up a story around this framework, and then presents and tells it together to the whole group.
