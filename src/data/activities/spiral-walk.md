instructions: "Done to recorded music. Start in a large circle, hands joined except on your (the leader's) left. Lead the circle to the left and into a spiral. Before the spiral gets too tight, reverse your direction and unwind. End in the original circle."
leadingTips: 'Ed Moyer opened his programs for special populations with the Spiral Walk; it appears first on each of his sample program plans in the book.'
