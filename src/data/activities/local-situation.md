instructions: |
  The material here is the dramatization of a situation or problem that is close to the group, that they can understand and for which they have real concern and feeling. These can be group problems, community problems, or even national problems if the group dramatizing them is emotionally affected. Strong feelings can be expressed through satire, burlesque, or real and sincere emotion. There is no law against being serious, however.

  This kind of skit can be used to dramatize a group gripe, and in so expressing it, relieve the pressure that it caused. It can present a problem to a larger group of people without lecturing; it can stimulate thought, discussion, and action; it can dramatize the need for a solution by presenting a comprehensive picture of the problem, not by solving it.
leadingTips: 'This kind of skit should also be fun, or it is apt not to be done at all.'
