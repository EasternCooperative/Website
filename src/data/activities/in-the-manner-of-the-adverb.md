instructions: |
  Players sit in a circle. One person volunteers to be the guesser and leaves
  the room, somewhere out of earshot. The rest of the group, coordinated by the
  leader, agrees on an adverb. Then they call the guesser back.

  The guesser picks one or more people in the circle to perform a very brief
  scene, less than ten seconds, "in the manner of the adverb." For example,
  "make dinner in the manner of the adverb." If the secret adverb is
  "carelessly," the performer might mime chopping vegetables and cutting
  themselves over and over.

  The guesser keeps calling for scenes until they are ready to guess, and may
  guess as many times as they like. The group tells them once they have it.
  Choose a new guesser and play another round, continuing until the leader ends
  the activity.
leadingTips: |
  If the guesser is struggling, offer a hint, or have the group signal when a
  guess is getting close.
