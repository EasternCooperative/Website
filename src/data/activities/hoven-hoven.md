lyrics: |
  *Chorus:*
  Ho'ven, ho'ven, gurry och ho, gurry och ho, gurry och ho.
  Ho'ven, ho'ven, gurry och ho, I've lost my darling baby-o.

  1. I left my darling lying here, lying here, lying here.
  I left my darling lying here
  To go and gather blaeberries.

  2. I found the trail of the mountain mist, mountain mist, mountain mist,
  I found the trail of the mountain mist
  But not a trace of baby-o.

  3. I found the track of the fawn in the woods, ...
  But not a trace of baby-o.

  4. I found the wee brown otter's tracks, ...

  5. I found the track of the swan on the lake, ...

  Sing the chorus after each verse. What other tracks or trails could be found?

  *Ho'ven and gurry och ho are terms of endearment.*

leadingTips: |
  For people who find singing hard at first, Dotti Siftar suggests starting with humming, then trying the gentle "Ho'ven, ho'ven, gurry och ho": feel the consonants on the lips and teeth, add a gentle rocking of the body from side to side, and let the song carry you to the others in the group.

  Sing the chorus after each verse, and ask the group what other tracks or trails could be found.
