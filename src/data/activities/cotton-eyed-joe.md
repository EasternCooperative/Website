title: 'Cotton-Eyed Joe'
type: 'dance'
draft: false
excerpt: 'An energetic American line dance: pump, pump, side-behind-side, then two-step around the room in lines that love a near-collision.'
origin: 'USA. Contributed by Ruth Sherman to Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990).'
instructions: |
  | Measures | |
  | --- | --- |
  | 1-4 | Pump, pump, 1, 2, 3. The right foot pushes down 2 times as if stepping on the brake pedal; then step right to the side, behind, side. Repeat with opposite footwork. |
  | 5-8 | Repeat the above. |
  | 9-16 | All do 2-steps (step-close-step) around the room: right forward, left close, right forward; left forward, right close, left forward. Continue 2-steps until the music for part 1 starts again, or 8 measures. |

  When the feet pump forward in the first part, bodies lean back.
leadingTips: 'A high-spirited fun dance, enjoyed especially by young (in spirit) people. It quickly creates a group feeling within the lines and often leads to improvised changes of direction. In the two-step, lines often have fun with "near-collisions" with other groups, or by someone at the end of a line suddenly backing up or moving forward in a circular fashion rather than going straight ahead. Let your groups experiment!'
formation: 'Lines of 2 to 7'
groupSizeAdaptationNotes: 'Dance in lines of 2-7 people; any number of lines.'
materialsNeeded: 'Music: Cotton-Eyed Joe (KIK-R 202 and others).'
