title: 'Elbow Tag'
type: 'game'
draft: false
excerpt: 'A tag game where the runner is safe by hooking onto a pair, which sends the far partner off running instead.'
origin: 'Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990)'
objective: 'To be safely attached to a partner.'
instructions: |
  **Formation:** 16 to 26 players. Players stand in pairs within designated boundary lines. Each pair hooks either right or left elbows, so that the partners face opposite directions. The other elbow is also bent, with the hand on the hip.

  One player is designated as a "runner" and another player as the "chaser." Other players stand attached in pairs as described above. The chaser tries to catch the runner, while the runner attempts to get safe by hooking an arm into a free elbow before being tagged by the chaser. As soon as the runner is hooked on, the third person on the other side in that set becomes the runner and the chaser now tries to catch this new player.

  When the chaser tags the runner, the roles are reversed, and the new runner now tries to get safe by hooking on.
leadingTips: 'In order to discourage endless chasing by only two players while the rest watch, the leader can circumscribe the boundaries so that players begin to rely more on strategy than speed.'
formation: 'Scattered pairs'

groupSizeMax: 26
