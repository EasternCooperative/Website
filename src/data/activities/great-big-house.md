instructions: |
  **Formation:** Single circle of couples facing in. Girls are on the right of their partners.

  1. All join hands and circle to the left with a walking step (16 counts): "Great big house..."
  2. All drop hands. Ladies go toward the center 4 steps, join hands, and simulate dipping water: stoop toward the floor and lift up (8 counts): "Old mill stream..."
  3. Men go toward the center four steps, reach over the joined hands of the ladies, and join hands in front of them. Men raise their joined hands over the heads and backs of the ladies. Ladies swing joined hands over the heads and backs of the men, forming a basket circle (8 counts): "Put one arm..."
  4. Break the circle. Each man swings the lady on his right twice around, leaving her on his right (8 counts): "Fare thee well..."
  5. Each man swings the lady on his left twice around, leaving her on his right. In this manner he progresses to a new partner each time (8 counts).

  The action is repeated as desired.

  Fare thee well, my pretty little miss, fare thee well, my daughter,
  Fare thee well, my darling wife with golden slippers on her.
