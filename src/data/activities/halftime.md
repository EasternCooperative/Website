title: 'Halftime'
type: 'game'
draft: false
excerpt: 'A challenging improv game where performers replay the same scene again and again, each pass in half the time of the one before, until it lasts only a few seconds.'
origin: 'Improv folk tradition'
relatedLeaderIds: ['isaac-lebwohl-steiner']
instructions: |
  Performers play a scene once, all the way to its natural conclusion. It works
  best when the scene has a lot of movement around the stage, often with travel
  built into the plot. The leader times this first performance.

  Each performance after that gets a time limit equal to half the previous one.
  If the first scene ran four minutes, the second must fit in two, the third in
  one, and so on. Each pass, the performers try to hit all the most important
  plot points, such as the locations visited and the challenges overcome, and
  cut the filler.

  The leader decides which round is the last, usually whichever one comes out to
  a single digit of seconds.
leadingTips: |
  Emphasize moving from place to place on stage.

  Teach basic stage blocking: face the audience, especially when speaking, and
  do not stand in front of another performer.
variations: |
  Start with a set initial time limit instead of timing a free first
  performance.
