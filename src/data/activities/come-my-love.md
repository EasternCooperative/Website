instructions: |
  **Formation:** A circle of partners faced for marching, men on the inside, women on their right.

  1. During the singing of the first verse, promenade in a circle, counter-clockwise.
  2. At the beginning of the second verse players drop hands, and the inside circle reverses direction: the file of women continues marching counter-clockwise while the men march clockwise.
  3. On the words "You're just about right" in verse 3, all take new partners, and joining both hands, swing around in place. Resume promenade position and repeat from the beginning, with new partners.
lyrics: |
  1. Come, my love, and go with me,
  Come, my love, and go with me,
  Come, my love, and go with me,
  And I will take good care of thee.

  2. You are too young, you are not fit, (3 times)
  You cannot leave your mother yet.

  3. You're old enough, you're just about right, (3 times)
  I asked your mother last Saturday night.
