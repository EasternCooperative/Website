origin: 'Improv folk tradition'
relatedLeaderIds: ['isaac-lebwohl-steiner']
instructions: |
  Participants stand or sit in a circle. One person begins by pointing at
  someone, either beside them or across the circle, and saying **"Zip."** That
  person now holds the energy and passes it on, pointing at anyone in the circle
  and saying **"Zap."** The third person receives it and passes it along with
  **"Zop."** The sequence then repeats from "Zip," and play continues until the
  leader calls an end.

  It works well at the start of a workshop as a warm-up, and as a low-barrier
  game for all-ages, program-for-all settings.
leadingTips: |
  Demonstrate the full Zip, Zap, Zop cycle yourself before starting. Emphasize
  making eye contact with the person you pass the energy to, since that is what
  keeps the pass clear and keeps everyone tracking who has it. Model high energy
  so the group matches it.
variations: |
  For a higher-energy version, players clap their hands together toward the next
  person instead of pointing.
