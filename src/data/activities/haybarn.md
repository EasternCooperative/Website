instructions: |
  Players stand or sit in a circle. The first player enthusiastically says
  "Yee-haw!" and swings a closed fist across the front of their body toward a
  neighbor. That neighbor now has the energy and passes it on the same way, in
  the same direction.

  The other option is to say "Haybarn!" while holding both hands over your head
  in an angled roof shape. A "Haybarn!" switches the direction of the energy,
  sending it back to the neighbor who just passed it. You cannot Haybarn a
  Haybarn.

  Play continues until the leader ends the activity.
