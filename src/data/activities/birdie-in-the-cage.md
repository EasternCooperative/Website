excerpt: 'A visiting-couple square dance: the first couple visits each couple in turn for "birdie in the cage" and "the crow hops in."'
origin: 'Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990)'
instructions: |
  **The call**

  **1.** All eight balance and all eight swing. A left allemande and a right hand grand. Meet your partner and promenade eight till you come straight.
  **2.** First couple balance and swing, out to the right.
  **3.** Birdie in the cage and three hands round.
  **4.** Birdie hops out and the crow hops in.
  **5.** Crow hops out and...
  **6.** Four hands round and round you go, the lady go "si" and the gent go "do."
  **7.** On to the next. (7-11 repeat 3-6 with the third couple.)
  **12.** On to the next. (12-16 repeat 3-6 with the fourth couple.)
  **17.** Now you're home. All eight balance and all eight swing. A left allemande, and a right hand grand. Meet your partner and promenade eight till you come straight.

  The second, third, and fourth couples repeat 2 to 17 in turn.

  **What the dancers do**

  **1.** All eight balance and all eight swing.
  **2.** Balance and swing.
  **3-5.** The first couple walks to the second couple. The first lady stands in the center while the other three join hands around her and circle to the left. The first lady and first gentleman exchange places while the circle continues moving to the left. The first gentleman then joins hands in the circle with his partner on his right (16 counts).
  **6.** First and second couples do a "do si do."
  **7-11.** The first couple walks to the third couple and repeats 3, 4, 5, and 6.
  **12-16.** The first couple walks to the fourth couple and repeats 3, 4, 5, and 6.
  **17.** Balance and swing.
formation: 'Square of four couples'
