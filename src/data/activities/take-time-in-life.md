excerpt: 'A Liberian dance song with a repeating melody and simple, meaningful words: "take time in life, ''cause you got far way to go."'
origin: 'Liberia. Sung by R. Von Richards. Copyright 1962, CRS, Inc., from *Laat ons Almal Saam Sing*. Written up in Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990).'
instructions: "A dance song, with a drum keeping the rhythm (the book's sheet music gives the drum pattern)."
lyrics: |
  1. I was passing by, my brother called me in,
  And he said to me, "You better take time in life."
  People, people, people, take time in life
  'Cause you got far way to go.

  2. I was passing by, my uncle called me in,
  And he said to me, "My nephew, take time in life."
  Nephew, take time in life, nephew, take time in life,
  Nephew, take time in life 'cause you got far way to go.

  3. I was passing by, some people called me in,
  And they said to me, "My young man, take time in life."
  Young man, young man, young man, take time in life
  'Cause you got far way to go.
