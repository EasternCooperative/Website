excerpt: "A dance warm-up: everyone moves to the leader's drum, walking on a slow beat, running on a fast one, and freezing when it stops."
origin: 'Adapted from *Play to Music* by Shelley Gordon. Reprinted in Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990).'
instructions: 'The leader holds a tambourine, drum, or other percussion object and beats a steady, slow beat. The group walks anywhere in the room as individuals. When the leader changes the beat to double the speed, the players follow the beat by running. At any time the leader can stop and the players must freeze. Alternate the sequence of walk, run, stop in unpredictable patterns.'
leadingTips: 'Warm-ups to dancing go a long way toward establishing confidence and enthusiasm for what follows, for the leader as well as the group.'
