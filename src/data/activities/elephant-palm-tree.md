instructions: |
  Everyone stands in a circle with one person, the leader at first, in the
  middle as the It. The It points to someone in the circle and names a shape,
  for example **"Elephant."** That person is the central figure of a
  three-person shape, and their two neighbors complete it with them.

  As soon as the It names the shape, they count out loud from one to five. If
  the It reaches five before all three players have formed the shape as it was
  taught, the It swaps places with whoever made the mistake, and that person
  becomes the new It. If the trio finishes the shape in time, the It moves on
  and points to a new central person.

  Play continues, with new Its rotating in as mistakes happen, until the leader
  ends the game.
leadingTips: |
  The It has "total It power." If the It decides someone made a mistake, then
  that person made a mistake and is the new It.

  Model counting slowly at first, then faster as people get the hang of it, or
  whenever the It wants to hand off being It.

  If the group is high energy and confident and the game is going to run past
  about eight minutes, pick and add extra Its depending on group size. This
  gets chaotic fast, and with the right group it is a lot of fun.
variations: |
  Shapes you can call:

  - **Elephant.** The center person puts their right arm out straight, wraps
    their left arm under the right so the right elbow sits in the crook of the
    left arm, grabs their nose with their left hand, and makes their best
    elephant trumpet noise. The two side people form the ears by making large
    "C" shapes with their arms, pointing toward the center person.
  - **Palm Tree.** The center person is the trunk, standing straight with both
    arms up above their head and wrists limp for the top fronds. Each side
    person chooses to be either a frond, waving their arms gently away from the
    center person, or a coconut, crouching down in a ball.
  - **Little Mermaid.** The center person is the mermaid. The two side people
    each hold one of the mermaid's hands while the center person leans forward
    and sings "Part of Your World." The side people then release their hands so
    the center person moves forward, and all three say "Splash" as it happens.
  - **DJ.** The center person is the DJ and the two side people are the
    turntables, leaning over in front of the DJ to form a table. The DJ
    scratches the record on the table.
  - **Improv.** For very advanced groups, the It can call a shape with no
    established model. It is up to the trio to invent the shape and up to the
    It to decide whether it counts.
