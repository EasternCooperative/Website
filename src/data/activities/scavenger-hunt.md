instructions: |
  There are many kinds of scavenger hunts that can be focused on outdoor discoveries. Pick a specific area of concentration and draw up a list accordingly, or use the following one. Find one or two things that:

  - smell good
  - smell bad
  - feel soft
  - feel hard
  - look pretty
  - look ugly

  Decide which found objects can be taken back to a central place. This decision depends upon whether removing them will significantly disrupt the ecosystem from which they came: Are there many more, so that the removal of one will not upset the balance? Will removal deface the area? Will it be missed?

  Then show and tell each other about the found objects.
leadingTips: 'This activity is a good way to see the diversity of the natural world.'
