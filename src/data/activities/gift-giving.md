excerpt: 'A low-barrier improv game that teaches making your partner look good and daring to be average, as players mime giving a gift and the receiver decides what it was.'
origin: 'Improv folk tradition'
relatedLeaderIds: ['isaac-lebwohl-steiner']
instructions: |
  Players sit or stand in a circle, usually seated. The leader models giving a
  gift to their neighbor, using object work to show the rough size, shape, or
  weight of the gift, whether it is a box, a ball, or a squirming animal. The
  giver does not say what the gift is.

  The receiver mimes opening it, then tells the giver what the gift was and
  thanks them for it enthusiastically. The giver accepts that and responds in
  kind. Play continues around the circle until everyone has given a gift.
leadingTips: |
  Every gift received is amazing and the receiver loves it. The giver accepts
  whatever the receiver names as the offer for the scene and responds
  accordingly.
variations: |
  Play in pairs, or in a line instead of a circle.
