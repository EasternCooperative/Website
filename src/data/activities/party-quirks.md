instructions: |
  Before leading this game, the leader collects or writes a set of quirks on
  small scraps of paper and puts them in a hat or similar container so players
  can draw at random. Example quirks: "Afraid of people shorter than them." or
  "Slowly turning into a giraffe."

  The group chooses a volunteer to be the host, who is the guesser. The rest of
  the players form a line offstage and secretly draw their quirks. The host
  leaves the room, somewhere out of earshot, so the guests can share their
  quirks with the audience, then returns.

  The host briefly prepares for the party, miming setting up food or
  decorations. After a few seconds the first guest knocks or rings the
  doorbell. A minute or two later the next guest enters, and so on. The host
  interacts with the guests and makes as many guesses as they like for each
  one. A guest who is guessed leaves the stage. Usually every guest is on stage
  before the first one leaves, but not always.
leadingTips: |
  Remind players not to talk over each other. When the host is dealing with one
  or two guests, the guests in the background should switch to silent miming
  rather than talking or making noise.

  Watch the blocking. Speak toward the audience and do not stand in front of
  another player.
variations: |
  Do not have the host leave, and keep the quirks secret from the audience too,
  so they can play along and guess.
