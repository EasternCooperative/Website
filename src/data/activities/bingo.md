origin: 'Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990)'
instructions: |
  **Formation:** Double circle, partners side by side and arm in arm, girl on the right.

  1. All walk counter-clockwise around the circle.
  2. All join hands to form one large, single circle, girls on partners' right and still walking counter-clockwise.
  3. Partners face each other and clasp right hands, singing "B." All pass to the next person giving the left hand, singing "I." Continue to a third person with the right hand, singing "N." Then on to the fourth person with the left hand, singing "G." Instead of the right hand to the fifth person, sing "O" and give your new partner a hug or a swing.

  Repeat from the beginning with the new partner.
lyrics: |

  And Bingo was his name.
  B-I-N-G-O!
