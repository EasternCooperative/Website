origin: 'Improv folk tradition'
relatedLeaderIds: ['isaac-lebwohl-steiner']
instructions: |
  Participants sit in trios, facing each other. The trio or the leader chooses
  the first translator. The other two play alien diplomats.

  One diplomat says several sentences of gibberish, directed at the other
  diplomat. The translator then says whatever they like, in a language everyone
  understands, as the "translation." The second diplomat responds in a
  different gibberish language, and the translator renders that too.

  Play continues until the leader calls for a switch. The game generally runs at
  least until everyone in the trio has had a turn as translator.
leadingTips: |
  Model the game in front of the group before starting.
variations: |
  Play in groups of four, where each diplomat has their own translator.
