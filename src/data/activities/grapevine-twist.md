instructions: |
  **Basic figure** (begin with the head couple going to the second couple):

  > "Take that lady by the wrist
  > And around that lady with a grapevine twist.
  > Into the center with a circle C.
  > And around that lonely gent you see.
  > Circle up four. On to the next."

  Repeat from the beginning, and as you collect couples in the line, the call becomes "Circle up six," then "Circle up eight 'til you get straight."

  The call resumes with the second couple going to the third; complete the call. Break. Third couple to fourth. Break. Fourth couple to first. End with break figures.

  **Break figures:** at the beginning, between figures, and at the end, do one or more break figures, such as:

  - Circle left
  - Circle right
  - Do si do your corner
  - Do si do your partner
  - Walk single file
  - Promenade (around the square to home)
  - Into the center and back ("Into the center with a great big yell. Do it again, you did it so well.")
formation: 'Square of four couples'
