excerpt: 'A Yiddish folk song of making do: the old overcoat becomes a jacket, a vest, a tie, a button, nothing, and finally a song.'
origin: 'Original Yiddish: "Hobikh Mir a Mantl." English translation by Teddi Schwartz and Arthur Kevess, from *Tumbalalaika: 17 Jewish Songs for Singing in English*, copyright 1956, renewed 1988, Teddi Schwartz, New York, NY. Used by permission in Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990).'
lyrics: |
  1. I had a little overcoat, as old as could be,
  Tra la la la la la la la la la la.
  What I'd ever do with it I just couldn't see,
  Tra la la la la la la la la la la.
  So I thought a little while, and made myself a jacket in the very latest style.
  Tra la la la la la, la la la la la la la,
  Made a jacket in the very latest style.

  2. jacket, vest
  3. vest, tie
  4. tie, button
  5. button, nothing
  6. nothing, song

  (In each verse, the first word replaces "overcoat" and the second replaces "jacket.")

leadingTips: '"I Had a Little Overcoat" is about repeated resilience in the face of adversity, and about satisfaction derived from creative problem-solving. For instance, what can you do when you have two children and only one egg? Make an omelet! And when you are down to absolutely nothing? Well, you can always make a song out of it. (Teddi Schwartz)'
