title: 'Hands Down Fifty-Four'
type: 'game'
draft: false
excerpt: 'A clapping chant sets the category, then each player names one item on the beat, with no repeats and no hesitations.'
origin: 'Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990)'
objective: 'To name an item in the category chosen, at the right time and in the rhythm.'
instructions: |
  **Formation:** 10-20 players in a circle. Each player holds left palm up; the right palm of each player is placed, palm down, on the left hand of the next player. All the words are said in this position.

  Each round of the game begins with this chant, said in unison. Between phrases, players clap twice. The chant gives the rules of the game.

  > "Hands down, 54" (clap, clap)
  > "On the list" (clap, clap)
  > "Names of" (clap, clap)
  > (first player names a category, such as colors, furniture, etc.) (clap, clap)
  > "One of each" (clap, clap)
  > "No repeats" (clap, clap)
  > "No hesitations" (clap, clap)
  > "So let's go." (clap, clap)
  > (first player names the first item in the category) (clap, clap)
  > (player to the left is next to name an item) (clap, clap)
  > (third player names an item and play continues around the circle to the left)

  When an error is made (repetition, someone can't think of an item, etc.) the game starts again with "Hands Down 54." The player following the one who missed is the one to name the new category and the first item in it.
