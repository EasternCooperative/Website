title: 'Follow the Leader Marching'

excerpt: 'A dance warm-up march in single or double file, where "change leaders" sends the leader to the back of the line.'
origin: 'Adapted from *Play to Music* by Shelley Gordon. Reprinted in Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990).'
instructions: |
  Accompanied by a drum, a rhythm band, singing, or recorded music (square tunes or marches), lead a march in single or double file. This can also be done with several leaders taking their lines in various directions but assuming responsibility to avoid collisions.

  When the leader calls "change leaders," the next in line assumes leadership and the last leader goes to the back of the line. Other body motions may be added at will.
