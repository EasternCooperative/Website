title: 'Kho'

excerpt: 'A chase through a circle of players facing alternately in and out, where the chaser can tag a circle player to take over the pursuit.'
origin: 'Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990)'
objective: 'For the chaser and the group to catch the runner; for the runner to escape capture.'
instructions: |
  **Formation:** 15-20 players in a circle, alternately facing the center and facing out. A chaser and a runner.

  The runner may run in and out of the circle between the players. The chaser must stay either inside or on the outside, i.e. wherever he or she started.

  The chaser may tap any player to continue the pursuit and takes that person's place. For example, if the chaser is outside and the runner has just run into the center of the circle, the chaser taps one of the players facing in, this player continues the chase, and the chaser steps into the vacated position.

  When the runner is tagged, the roles reverse.
leadingTips: 'Competition is necessary to make the game work, but the roles of all the players change so often that the interest is much more on the changing roles than on who occupies them, so there is little consequence to being caught (Sioux Free).'
