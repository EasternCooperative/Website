excerpt: 'A Hungarian folk song about two brothers: as each one leaves, his name drops out and the group sings silence in its place.'
origin: 'Hungarian folk song. Translated by Suzy Sann Harsanyi, copyright 1957, C.R.S., Delaware, Ohio. Written up in Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990).'
lyrics: |
  1. Hajmási Péter, Hajmási Pál,
  Hajmási Péter, Hajmási Pál,
  Hajmási Péter, Hajmási Pál,
  Hajmási Péter, Hajmási Pál.

  2. Off went our Péter, home stayed our Pál.
  Hajmási (silent), Hajmási Pál. (3 times)

  3. Off went our Péter, off went our Pál.
  Hajmási (silent), Hajmási (silent).
language: 'Hungarian names, English verses'
translation: '"Hajmási" (High-mah-she) is the family name; in Hungary the family name comes first. Péter and Pál are brothers; when one is absent, his name is silent.'
