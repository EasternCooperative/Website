origin: 'From *Songs to Sing and Sing Again* by Shelley Gordon, copyright 1984 Shelley Gordon. Reprinted in Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990).'
instructions: |
  With recorded music (in a 4/4 beat) as background, the leader does a series of motions and sounds for the group to follow. For example:

  | Count | 1 | and | 2 | and | 3 | and | 4 | and |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | | Clap | | Clap | | Snap | | Snap | |
  | | Snap | Snap | | Clap | | Clap | Clap | |
  | | Clap | | Clap | | Snap (right) | | Snap (left) | |

  Possibilities are endless. Use tongue, cheeks, chest (drumming), shoulders, arms, bending, stretching, etc.
leadingTips: 'Some members may prefer to improvise and should be encouraged, provided they stay with the beat.'
materialsNeeded: 'Recorded music in a 4/4 beat.'
