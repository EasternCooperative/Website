title: 'Péteca'
type: 'game'
draft: false
excerpt: 'A Brazilian keep-it-up game: the group bats a light ball skyward with open palms, calling out the alphabet one letter per hit.'
origin: 'Brazil, where boys make a cone-shaped leather peteca filled with sand, with feathers tied in the upper end. Written up in Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990).'
objective: 'To keep the ball in the air.'
instructions: |
  **Formation:** 5-12 players per group. Players stand in a cluster. Each group has a light ball or large balloon.

  The ball is tossed into the air, with individual players striking it upward with the palm of the hand. Any player may strike the ball at any time, but never two times in succession. Each time the ball is sent upward, the group shouts out the next letter of the alphabet. When the ball drops to the ground, the group begins again from "A".
leadingTips: "Péteca is a cooperative game: the group competes only against the game's obstacles, and getting farther into the alphabet each time is the success. Nobody is singled out when the ball drops; the group just starts again (Sioux Free)."
formation: 'Clusters of 5 to 12'
