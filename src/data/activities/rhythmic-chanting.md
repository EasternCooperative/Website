excerpt: 'Four groups chant words of one, two, three, and four syllables on a common beat until all four layers are going at once.'
origin: 'From *Songs to Sing and Sing Again* by Shelley Gordon, copyright 1984 Shelley Gordon. Reprinted in Of Play and Playfulness (ECRS, 1990).'
instructions: |
  Divide the group into four sections. Each section is assigned (or chooses) a word of one, two, three, or four syllables taken from one category; for example: names of flowers, vegetables, places, games, people.

  | Group 1 | peas | peas | peas | peas |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Group 2 | carrots | carrots | carrots | carrots |
  | Group 3 | celery | celery | celery | celery |
  | Group 4 | rutabaga | rutabaga | rutabaga | rutabaga |

  As the leader conducts the common beat 1, 2, 3, 4, the groups enter one at a time until all are chanting in unison. (Note that each word fits into one beat.)
variations: 'Ostinati may be added by each group (see Companion Rounds with Rhythmic Ostinati).'
