FD - Armstrong, Don. The Caller/Teacher Manual for Contras. Sets in Order. American Square Dance Society, 462 N. Robertson, Los Angeles, CA 90048.

CA - Atwater, Mary. Byways in Handweaving. Second Printing. MacMillan, New York, 1972. Basic directions for many methods of weaving.

G - Aycox, Frank. Games We Should Play in School. Front Row Experience, 540 Discovery Bay Blvd., Byron, CA 94514, 1985. Many familiar games, with instructions, comments. Teaching guides. Sections on leadership, philosophy and classroom situations.

P - Batchelder, M. Puppets and Plays. Harper and Row, New York, 1956. Out of print: available in some libraries. "Still the best": H.K.

FD - Beliatus, Vytautus B. Merrily Dance. 5790 Highway 80 South, Burnsville, NC, World Around Songs. A small collection of ethnic dances from Middle Europe: includes directions.

S - Blood-Patterson, Peter, Editor. Rise Up Singing. Sing Out Corporation, P.O. Box 5253, Bethlehem, PA 18015. A wordbook listing songs under subject matter, with title index and guitar chords. Melody lines not included.

G - Boyd, Neva. Handbook of Recreational Games. Dover Publications, Inc.. 1973, New York. More than 300 games, compiled by one of the first educators to view play as a path to social growth.

L - Boyd, Neva L. Play and Game Theory in Group Work. University of Illinois, 1971. A selection of essays on the theory of play and games.

FD - Casey, Betty. International Folk Dancing U.S.A. Doubleday & Co., Inc., Garden City, NY, 1981. An excellent resource book of folk dance information from the people and the countries that have influenced the growth of international folk dancing in this country. More than 60 authors, teachers, leaders and folk dancers have contributed their knowledge and historical perspective.

PI, FD - Chase, Richard. Singing Games and Play Party Games. Dover Publications, New York. Traditional games and dances from the Appalachian mountains. Music included.

FD - Community Dance Manuals, Books 1-7. Douglas Kennedy, Series Editor, with new intro. by Tony Parkes. Princeton Book Company, Publishers, P.O. Box 109, Princeton, NJ 08542. Available from Country Dance Society. Dance directions for traditional English dances, mostly contemporary, with printed music for suggested tunes. Highly recommended for musicians or leaders.

L - Cooper, Susan and Cathy Heenan. Preparing, Designing, Leading Workshops. Van Nostrand Reinhold, 1980.

S - Cope, Nan. Bright Morning Stars. World Around Songs, 5790 Highway 80 South, Burnsville, NC 28714.

FD - Country Dance and Song Society of America. 12 New South St., Northampton, MA 01060.

CA - D'Amico, Victor, Frances Wilson and Moreen Maser. Art for the Family. Museum of Modern Art, New York. Invaluable book, but out of print.

CA - Edwards, Betty. Drawing on the Right Side of the Brain. J.P. Tardier Inc., Los Angeles, CA, 1979.

D - Elkind, Samuel. Improvisation Handbook. Foresman and Co., Glenview, IL. 1975. Many ideas for starting activities for informal dramatics. Improvisation requires a group effort which depends on cooperation, support and trust among players. Its course is unplanned and open-ended.

FD - Everett, Bert. Traditional Canadian Dances. Can-Ed Media, 185 Spadina Ave., Toronto, Canada. 35 squares and other useful dances, plus calling tips.

FD - Farwell, Jane. Folk Dances for Fun. World Around Songs, 5790 Hwy. 80 South, Burnsville, NC 28714. Ethnic dances with directions and leadership ideas.

L - Flynn, Elizabeth and John LaFaso. Group Discussion as a Learning Process. Paulist Press. 1972.

G - Fluegelman, Andrew, Editor. The New Games Book. Dolphin Books/Doubleday and Company, Inc., Garden City, New York, 1976.

G - More New Games. Dolphin Books/Doubleday and Company, Inc., Garden City, New York, 1976. These two books have a somewhat different approach to playing games, with lots of good ideas and techniques, a spirit of fun and a solid foundation of cooperative, non-competitive and all-inclusive play.

PI, S, G - Fowke, Edith. Sally Go Round the Sun. McClelland and Stewart, Toronto. 30 children's songs, rhymes and games.

FD - Fowke, Edith and Richard Johnson. Folk Songs of Canada. Waterloo Music Company, Waterloo, Ontario, Canada. Large collection of songs native to Canada and suitable for group singing.

CA - Frank, Frederick. The Zen of Seeing: Seeing/Drawing as Meditation. Random Press, New York. 1975.

S, PI, G, FD - Gordon, Shelley. Play to Music. C/o author, 25 Belsize Drive, Toronto, Ontario, Canada M4S 1L3. Dances, play parties, games and songs. Leadership information focuses on the playful approach to music.

PI, S - Gordon, Shelley. "Put More Music Into Play" in Paul F. Wilkinson (ed), In Celebration of Play. Croom Helm Ltd., London. 1980.

S, P1 - Gordon, Shelley. Songs to Sing and Sing Again. World Around Songs, 5790 Highway 80 South, Burnsville, NC 28714. Pocketsized booklet designed to aid group singing. Large collection of songs and rhythmic and dramatic activities.

FD - Handy Folk Dance Book, Handy Square Dance Book, and Handy Play Party Book. World Around Songs, 5790 Highway 80 South, Burnsville, NC 28714. The first two are out of print, but still available in libraries and personal collections. HPPB, in a new edition, is available.

O, P, CA - Hughes, Langston. The First Book of Rhythms. A book that stimulates a sense of design.

L - Huizinga, Johan. Homo Ludens, A Study of the Play Element in Culture. Beacon Press, Boston, MA. 1964.

FD - Kraus, Richard. Folk Dancing. MacMillan Company, NY.

G, FD, O, L - Kraus, Richard. Recreation Leader's Handbook. McGraw Hill, NY, NY. 1955. A variety of recreation materials and discussion of leadership.

FD - Lapson, Dvora. Dances of the Jewish People. Jewish Education Committee, 426 W. 58th St., NY, NY. Israeli and Eastern European dances with music.

G - LeFevre, Dale. New Games for the Whole Family. Putnam Publishing Group. New York, 1988.

FD - Linnell, Rod. Square Dances from a Yankee Caller's Clipboard. Copyright 1974 by The New England Square Dance Caller, P.O.B. NC, Norwell, MA 02061 (currently out of print). A collection of contras and squares and double quadrilles, with excellent discography and notes on calling the dances.

CA - Mattil, Edward. Meaning in Crafts. 6th printing, Prentice-Hall, Englewood Cliffs, NJ, 1964. Many crafts, presented in an open-ended approach which allows for maximum creative activity.

G - Orlick, Terry. Cooperative Sports and Games, Book I and Book II. Random House, Missisauga.

CA - Schuman, Jo Miles. Art from Many Hands. Prentice-Hall, Englewood Cliffs, NJ. Craft ideas from many different cultures.

CA - Stribling, Mary Lou. Art from Found Materials. Crown Press. 1970.

D - Taylor, Loren E. Pantomime and Pantomime Games. Burgess Publishing. Minneapolis, MN.

FD - Tolman and Page. The Country Dance Book. Stephen Greene Press, Brattleboro, VI. Re-print of 1937 edition. Instructions for New England dances, and anecdotes.

FD - Vouras, Mary and Ricky Holden. Greek Dances. Folkcraft Press, 10 Fenwick, Newark, NJ 07114.

D - Way, Brian. Development Through Drama. Longman, London & Toronto. Basic approach to leading creative dramatic activities.

CA - Whyte, Kathleen. Design in Embroidery. Batsford, London. Excellent book of stimulating ideas and exercises to develop one's sense of design in arts and crafts, not just embroidery.

CA - Wilson, Jean. Weaving Is for Anyone. Reinhold, NY. Variety of ideas and techniques for off-loom weaving.
