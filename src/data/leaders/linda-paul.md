bio: |
  Linda holds a PhD in Developmental Psychology from the University of Kansas and an M.Ed. in Educational Leadership from Bank Street College of Education. After teaching at the University of Alberta and on Long Island, NY, she found her true calling as a school administrator in special and early childhood education. During her 21 years at one school, she started classes that integrated children with and without special needs. Now retired in St. Petersburg, Florida, Linda volunteers in a kindergarten class, advocates for children in foster care, and is a museum docent. She loves walking in local parks, photographing flora and fauna, and traveling.
