bio: Rob Eames, M.A., is a Certified Trainer with the [Center for Nonviolent Communication](https://www.cnvc.org/trainers/rob-eames) and a Relationship Coach who specializes in helping people build relationships rooted in authenticity & compassion. As founder of [Couples Focus](https://www.couplesfocus.com/) and [Relational Academy](https://courses.relationalacademy.com/courses), Rob offers coaching and workshops that blend practical skillbuilding with experiential discovery. His approach centers on cultivating *relational skills*—both with ourselves and others—as the foundation for interdependence, resilience, and meaningful connection.
