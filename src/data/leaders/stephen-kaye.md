bio: |
  Having learned Spoon Carving from Reinaldo Cruz at Camp Winni Steve returned to camp each summer to carve, whether there was a class or not. A neighbor, Vivekanand Pandey Vimal, had a tree fall and saved the wood, hoping someone would "honor" his magnificent fallen tree. So Steve began showing Vivek his wood carving. Their friendship grew, and the Sunday Wood Group was born. The group grows and changes, with new people joining alongside old friends. Just about every weekend, a group comes together to chat and carve. Steve continues to develop his teaching skills every weekend and hopes to bring that to ECRS Winter Adventure.
